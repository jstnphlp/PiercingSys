System Observations Item 3: Calendar Ends at 11:59 PM

Review-only prototype for /app?view=calendar. Production files are not modified. The proposed behavior keeps 60px hourly rhythm, extends the visible grid to the midnight boundary internally, and labels the bottom boundary as 11:59 PM with no 12 AM row.

Recommendation

Use calendarStartMinutes = 8 * 60, calendarEndMinutes = 24 * 60, hourHeight = 60, and calendarHeight = (end - start) * pixelsPerMinute = 960px. Render hourly labels from 8 AM through 11 PM, then render a separate end marker at the bottom as 11:59 PM.
